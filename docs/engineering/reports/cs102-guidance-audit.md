## Observed implementation conflicts and resolution

1. `src/domain/shopping/putAway.ts` calls `canonicalIngredientName(source.name)` directly; Shopping calls `purchaseDisplayName`/`ingredientStructure` before identity. This is a consumer mismatch, not merely oversized CSS. Resolve with shared lossless projection for current AND legacy saved purchases. Preserve original text and source receipts.
2. `ShoppingPutAway.tsx` renders an editable name field for every preselected row. That satisfies ability-to-correct but conflicts with Principles6/8/10. Show compact prefilled rows and optional Change; one batch Apply. Safe auto-selection stays.
3. Current put-away RPC deliberately marks availability and retains null amounts. Owner now approved measured stock. Add actual confirmed quantity receipts; do not infer quantities for historical receipts or turn null stock into zero.
4. Quantity unknown is not permission for repeated mandatory stocktake questions. Safe identity/location can still be recognised; uncertainty stays explicit with optional correction/skip and no fabricated measured stock.
5. `canonicalIngredientName` v1 is deliberately immutable/limited. Evolve the shared consumer projection with version/parity tests instead of changing historical receipt identity or blanket-stripping product qualifiers. Fresh/minced garlic is distinct from jarred paste, powder or commercially minced garlic; green/red capsicum, baby spinach, dietary/form qualifiers remain.
6. Orchard says preserve photo placeholder during its old reskin, but this task explicitly authorises compact Plan behaviour. Remove empty decorative space where it obstructs titles; preserve real imagery and accessibility. Do not rebuild unrelated Home/navigation.
7. General architecture mentions staging/zero-downtime sequence; owner explicitly accepted private-MVP maintenance → merge → protected DB/Edge → reopen. Preserve that exception without introducing new release gates; no merge/deploy authority here.
8. CS23/22 are Done despite stale Backlog/readiness text. Preserve historical statuses; CS102 owns verified gaps rather than treating Done/public smoke as feature proof.

## Concrete user-journey acceptance checklist

- [ ] Bought known ingredients open as compact readable clean product names, recognised existing Pantry destination, known amount/unit and sensible location; no routine required text entry.
- [ ] Shared parser handles legacy labels too: freshly minced garlic retains prep separately; duplicated capsicum token is not a new Pantry identity; handfuls are a measure, not part of a spinach name. Preserve fresh/baby/colour/form distinctions and honest uncertainty.
- [ ] A safe batch needs one confirmation after opening; optional Change exposes edit/exclude controls. A genuinely ambiguous match is isolated and does not hold ordinary items hostage.
- [ ] Actual purchase amount defaults from trusted bought snapshot, not full recipe demand or guessed pack size; user may adjust it. Unknown existing total is not silently incremented from zero.
- [ ] Plan600g+400g, Pantry500g → buy500g; buying500g then putting away500g yields stock1000g and no duplicate purchase. Done600g leaves400g and outstanding400g demand covered; Undo restores recorded600g once.
- [ ] Unknown units remain a check. Jarred/powder/fresh and allergy variants never silently merge. No paid AI calls.
- [ ] Done freezer meal uses reserved portions only; manual/leftover meal does not deduct fresh ingredients twice. Move/edit/delete/retry respect occurrence identity.
- [ ] At320px and200% zoom, Plan titles remain readable, tick/menu/actions reachable; put-away commit visible/reachable without three large form cards filling the modal. Keyboard focus, Escape, announcements and axe checked.
- [ ] Refresh/response loss/concurrent household edits do not double-apply. Undo after later stock changes uses recorded effects and reviewed current values. Household switch discards stale responses.
