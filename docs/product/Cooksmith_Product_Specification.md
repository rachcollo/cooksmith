# Cooksmith Product Specification

**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** Authoritative Product Specification

## Vision

Cooksmith is an intelligent household meal planning assistant that quietly removes the invisible work of feeding a household.

**Product Promise**

> Cooksmith does the thinking. The household makes the final decision.

## Goals

- Save people time
- Reduce mental load
- Reduce food waste
- Reduce grocery spend
- Make meal planning calm and simple

## Target Users

- Busy families
- Working couples
- Households wanting less effort around meal planning

## MVP Objectives

Users can:

1. Create a household
2. Configure preferences
3. Confirm pantry staples
4. Browse public recipes
5. Create private recipes
6. Build or generate a fortnight meal plan
7. Swap and lock meals
8. Generate a shopping list
9. Copy a Coles/Woolworths shopping export
10. Generate a meal preparation plan

## Weekly Journey

1. Welcome
2. Pantry Check
3. Plan
4. Prepare
5. Shop
6. Cook
7. Learn

## Product Principles

Refer to **Cooksmith_Product_Principles.md** as the governing document.

## Core Epics

- Household Management
- Pantry Management
- Recipe Library
- Meal Planning
- Meal Preparation
- Smart Shopping
- AI Intelligence
- Administration

## AI Philosophy

AI proposes.

The household decides.

Deterministic logic always controls allergies, permissions, calculations, pantry quantities and business rules.

## Friend Test Non-Goals

- Retailer API integration
- Barcode scanning
- OCR pantry scanning
- Native mobile apps
- Subscription billing
- Push notifications

## Success Criteria

- Onboarding under 10 minutes
- Fortnight planning under 15 minutes
- Shopping list generated in under 5 seconds

Cooksmith succeeds when users stop asking:

> "What's for dinner?"
